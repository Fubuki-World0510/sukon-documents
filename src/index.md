**Sukon Bot** is a multifunctional Discord bot designed to enhance server management and provide comprehensive utilities for your community. Built with performance and reliability in mind, it offers a complete solution for modern Discord servers.

- **Social Media Integration** - Automated notifications for new posts from YouTube, Twitter, and Facebook
- **Music System** - High-quality audio streaming and playlist management
- **Entertainment** - Anime content and specialized entertainment features
